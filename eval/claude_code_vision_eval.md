## Prompt Improvement Suggestions

### 1. Hallucinated Clothing and Accessory Details

**Pattern:** The model frequently invents specific clothing details — fabric patterns, colors, accessories — that are not clearly visible in the image. Examples:
- Photo 5 (_MG_6834): "tied back with a pink scarf" — it's a hair bow, not a scarf.
- Photo 13 (_MG_6284): Invented "pearl necklace" on woman; missed the prominent sunglasses both subjects are wearing.
- Photo 7 (IMG_1170): Invented "blue shirt and a red and blue hat" on Elmo costume.

**Recommendation:** Add to the prompt: "Describe only clothing and accessories that are clearly and prominently visible. Do not infer or speculate about items that are partially obscured or too small to read clearly. If you cannot clearly see a detail, omit it rather than guess."

---

### 2. Person Count Errors — Missing People in Frame

**Pattern:** When multiple people are in the scene, the model often focuses on one or two and ignores others, leading to wrong person counts. Examples:
- Photo 30 (IMG_1424): Two girls doing Easter egg hunt — desc mentions only one.
- Photo 61 (IMG_0678): Two children on ponies — desc describes only one.
- Photo 65 (IMG_4460): Adult + child walking — desc says "a man" only.

**Recommendation:** Add to the prompt: "Before writing your description, count every person visible in the frame, including background figures if they are clearly identifiable. State the total number of people at the start of the description (e.g., 'Two children and one adult...'). Do not omit any people who occupy more than 5% of the frame."

---

### 3. Catastrophic Loop / Repetitive Output (Model Failure)
