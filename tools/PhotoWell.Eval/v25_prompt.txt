Describe this photo in 2-3 sentences for a searchable personal photo library. Use gender-neutral language: person, people, child, adult. Never use man, woman, boy, girl, or gendered pronouns.

COMPOSITION: Identify ALL distinct subjects in the frame. If there are both people AND objects (cake, decorations, props), describe both. Don't focus only on one and ignore the rest. Example: "Three children sitting on a rug holding ice cream. A yellow cartoon character container is visible." NOT just "Children at an ice cream party" (inferred) or just "Ice cream" (ignoring people).

Start with concrete description: "A golden retriever", "A concert stage with performers", "A pink cake with a doll on top", "Three people on a rug". Never start with "This photo" or "The image".

Key rules:
- Describe visible objects and people clearly — don't invent details or decorative items you can't see
- For people: state count, positions (sitting/standing), visible clothing colors, what they're holding
- For objects: name them clearly (cake, pumpkins, ice cream) and key features you can actually see
- Activities: Only describe what's clearly visible (sitting, holding, standing). Don't infer "playing", "celebrating", "having fun" unless obviously happening
- Game pieces, figurines, toys, statues, dolls are objects, not people
- Backlit or shadowed subjects: describe silhouette/outline only — don't invent invisible features
- Animals: identify clearly (dog, cat) or say "a pet"
- Never repeat phrases. Use varied vocabulary.

Important: If confused or image is complex, describe what you can identify CLEARLY rather than guessing.

Example descriptions:
- "Three children sitting on a checkered rug. One child holds a yellow ice cream container. An adult sits nearby."
- "Two carved pumpkins with faces on a porch step. The pumpkins are orange and sit on weathered wood."
- "A pink layered cake with a doll in a pink dress on top. The cake is decorated with pink frosting and white candies."

Write in prose (no lists), present tense.
