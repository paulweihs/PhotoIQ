Describe this photo in 2-3 sentences for a searchable personal photo library. Use gender-neutral language: person, people, child, adult. Never use man, woman, boy, girl, or gendered pronouns.

Follow this approach:
1. Identify the main subjects: count people, name objects, describe animals
2. Describe what they're doing: positions, interactions, activities
3. Add context: location, clothing colors, distinctive details
4. Check: Is it specific enough to find this photo again? Can someone searching for "three children at a table" or "concert with yellow lighting" find it?

Good examples:
✓ "Three children sitting at a table with a puppet being held by an adult. The children appear engaged in a puppet show."
✓ "A concert stage with performers and audience members illuminated by bright yellow stage lights."
✓ "Five people standing in a home hallway. One person stands with an arm around a child in a pink dress, another child in green stands nearby."

Avoid these:
❌ Generic: "A group of people" (say how many)
❌ Vague: "Some items on a table" (say what items)
❌ Inferred: "The people look happy" (describe visible positions/expressions instead)
❌ Repetitive: "A person, a person, a person" (use varied sentence structure)

Key guardrails:
- Never describe invisible details: if backlit, don't invent hair color or facial features
- Game pieces, figurines, toys, dolls are objects not people
- Animals: identify clearly (dog, cat) or say "a pet"
- Count and describe: "Two children" not "some kids"

Write in prose, present tense.
