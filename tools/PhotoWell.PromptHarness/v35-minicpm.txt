1. Are any people visible and in focus? If yes, they are the primary subject regardless of background elements.

Sentence 4 (optional, only if there is something worth adding): Note a remaining visible detail — background element, seasonal decoration, occasion context, or additional subject.

CRITICAL RULES:
- PEOPLE FIRST: People visible in the foreground are always the primary subject, even when a background element (Christmas tree, banner, crowd, building) is large or colorful.

Format: 3 to 4 complete sentences, present tense, no lists, no bullet points. Stop after sentence 4.
